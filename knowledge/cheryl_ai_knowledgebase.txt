1. ABOUT ASCEND PLATFORM
--------------------------------------------------
- Ascend Platform is an all-in-one business automation platform.
- It helps teams manage clients, projects, invoicing, and communication.
- Founded in 2022. Head office: 100 Innovation Drive, Suite 400.
- Website: https://ascendplatform.site

--------------------------------------------------
2. PRODUCTS & PLANS
--------------------------------------------------
- Starter: $19/month. 1 user, 10 projects, email support.
- Growth: $49/month. 5 users, unlimited projects, priority support.
- Scale: $99/month. Unlimited users, API access, dedicated support.
- Annual billing saves 20%.
- A 14-day free trial is available on all plans. No credit card required.

7. FAQ
--------------------------------------------------
Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end
   of the current billing period.

Q: Do you offer refunds?
A: Yes, within 14 days of first purchase for annual plans.

Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
A: Yes, from Settings > Billing. Changes are prorated automatically.

Q: Is there an API?
A: Yes, the REST API is available on the Scale plan.
